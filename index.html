TopScore for Android

Privacy Policy - TopScore

Effective date: August 30, 2026; last updated: September 19, 2026

TopScore ("TopScore", "we", "us", or "our") is an unofficial football scores and statistics app. It provides fixtures, live scores, match details, standings, player and team information, comparisons, search, favorites, push notifications, and a home-screen widget. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the TopScore Android app and the choices available to you.

TopScore is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any football league, club, federation, or governing body.

Summary

  • No TopScore account or login is required.
  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information.
  • Your followed leagues, teams, and players and your display preferences are stored locally, subject to Android backup settings.
  • Search terms and requests for football content are sent to the TopScore API.
  • If you choose to enable match alerts, we use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver real-time notifications for goals, kickoffs, red cards, and match events.
  • The app displays banner and native ads using Google AdMob.
  • Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents and records advertising privacy choices before ads are requested.
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics are integrated to collect crash reports and aggregate app usage metrics for app stability and performance.

1. Information you provide

Search terms

When you use search, the words you enter are sent to the TopScore API so it can return matching clubs, players, and competitions. Search begins after at least two characters and a short pause in typing. Search terms are not used to create a user account or advertising profile by us. Please do not enter personal or sensitive information into the football search field.

Feedback email

If you choose Send Feedback, TopScore opens your email app with a draft addressed to us. The draft includes the TopScore version, Android version, Android SDK version, and device manufacturer and model to help us troubleshoot. You may review, edit, or delete this information before sending. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email. If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, attachments you choose to add, and the diagnostic details left in the draft.

2. Information stored on your device

TopScore stores the following app data locally:

  • IDs of leagues, teams, and players you follow;
  • theme mode and selected color palette;
  • notification preferences and recent event IDs to prevent duplicate alerts;
  • a developer-server address in debug/development builds, if configured;
  • cached football responses and images used to improve loading and limited offline availability; and
  • advertising consent status and choices stored on the device by Google's User Messaging Platform, together with temporary in-memory advertising state.

TopScore stores your followed leagues, clubs, and players locally on your device. We do not require a user account or maintain a personal user profile on our backend. However, when the app retrieves details for followed items, the relevant football IDs are included in those content requests. Additionally, if push notifications are enabled, followed club, match, and player IDs are synchronized alongside your device registration token to our notification service to route relevant match event notifications to your device.

Android backup is enabled for the app. Depending on your device, Android version, account, and backup settings, app preferences may be included in an encrypted Google backup or copied during device transfer. This process is controlled by Android and your backup provider, not by the TopScore backend. Installation-specific notification registration and pending-deletion state are excluded from Android backup and device transfer. See Google's Android backup information.

3. Information processed automatically

TopScore API requests

The app connects to https://topscore.tofalabs.com/api/ to obtain public football content. Requests can include dates, page and filter choices, and football identifiers such as a fixture, league, season, team, or player ID. Our server and hosting infrastructure may also automatically receive standard network metadata such as IP address, request time, requested URL, response status, and user-agent or similar device/network information. We use this information to deliver content, maintain availability, prevent abuse, secure the service, and diagnose technical problems.

Images and flags

Team crests, competition logos, player images, and country flags can be loaded directly from third-party asset hosts, including API-Sports media servers and FlagCDN. Those hosts receive the network information ordinarily needed to deliver a file, including your IP address, the requested image, and request metadata. Their handling of that information is governed by their own terms and privacy practices.

Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform

TopScore uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display banner and native advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • advertising identifiers, app-set identifiers, and other device identifiers where available;
  • device, operating system, language, network, and app information;
  • ad impressions, ad exposure, clicks, interactions, and advertising performance; and
  • diagnostic, fraud-prevention, and security information.

Where required by applicable privacy rules, TopScore uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to determine whether a privacy message is required, present advertising choices, and remember the user's selection. UMP may process consent status, app and device information, and regional applicability signals for these purposes. Ads are not requested until UMP indicates that ad requests are permitted under the configured message.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, limit repetition, detect fraud and abuse, and, where permitted, personalize advertising. Even non-personalized ads can use identifiers or local storage for frequency capping and aggregated reporting. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google's Advertising information.

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

TopScore uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app usage in aggregate and Firebase Crashlytics to detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and non-fatal technical errors. Depending on device settings and availability, these services may automatically process:

  • an app-instance identifier, Firebase installation identifier, Crashlytics installation UUID, and Advertising ID where available;
  • approximate location inferred from a masked IP address;
  • device model, operating system, app version, language, network, and other app or device metadata;
  • app lifecycle, session, screen-view, and interaction events;
  • crash stack traces, relevant app and device state, crash counts, and diagnostic information; and
  • developer-supplied diagnostic details, currently limited to the TopScore version and non-fatal UMP consent-flow error messages.

Crashlytics may also use Analytics breadcrumb events showing app actions that occurred shortly before a crash. TopScore does not set a Firebase user ID or intentionally attach your name, email address, football search terms, or favorites to Firebase reports. Firebase information is used for aggregate usage measurement, crash reporting, troubleshooting, and improving app stability. Learn more in Google's Privacy and Security in Firebase documentation and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the published Google privacy message includes consent for Analytics, the user's UMP selection is also interpreted through Google Consent Mode for Analytics storage. Available choices can be reviewed through TopScore Settings → Manage ad privacy when that entry is required.

Firebase Cloud Messaging (Push Notifications)

TopScore provides opt-in push notifications for live match events, such as goals, kickoffs, red cards, and full-time scores for matches, clubs, or players you follow.

Upon app launch, Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) service generates a technical device registration token, which the app normally registers with the TopScore API (https://topscore.tofalabs.com/api/). Generating and registering this technical token is separate from the Android notification display permission: match event notifications will only be posted to your screen if you grant the runtime notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS on Android 13+) and choose to follow matches, clubs, or players. After you use Delete notification data, the app suppresses registration with the TopScore backend for that installation until you turn Push Notifications on again. Google may still manage technical Firebase identifiers under its own lifecycle rules; this action does not delete Analytics or Crashlytics data.

When you select followed clubs, matches, or notification preferences, the app synchronizes the relevant football entity IDs (clubs, matches, players) with our backend so event messages can be properly routed. The FCM token is an arbitrary pseudonymous device identifier: it is not linked to your personal identity, name, or email, and is not used for advertising profiling. You can turn notifications off or customize alert categories at any time within TopScore Settings → Notifications or via Android system settings.

6. Permissions and device access

The app or its included advertising SDK uses these Android capabilities:

  • INTERNET: fetch football data and images, load ads, deliver push notifications, and open online content.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: detect network availability.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): display opted-in live match notifications, goal alerts, and kickoff alerts. You can grant, deny, or revoke this permission at any time in system settings.
  • AD_ID and Android Privacy Sandbox advertising capabilities: advertising, measurement, attribution, and related controls where available. These may be added to the final app manifest by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

TopScore does not request runtime access to precise or approximate device location, camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, call logs, SMS, photos, media, or files. The only runtime permission requested is POST_NOTIFICATIONS on Android 13 and newer, which is requested only if you choose to receive live match notifications.

7. Advertising choices

Your available advertising choices depend on your device, Google account, region, and applicable law. You can review ad personalization controls in Android settings and in Google My Ad Center. Android versions that provide an Advertising ID also let you reset or delete that identifier in system privacy or ads settings. Limiting personalization does not necessarily remove ads.

In regions where Google requires a privacy-options entry point, you can review or change your advertising choices from TopScore Settings → Manage ad privacy. Withdrawing consent can result in non-personalized or limited ads rather than removing advertising entirely.

8. Data retention

  • Local app data: remains until you change it, clear TopScore's storage, or uninstall the app, subject to any Android cloud backup or device-transfer copy.
  • API and infrastructure records: standard server and security records, if generated, are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for operations, security, abuse prevention, and legal obligations.
  • Feedback: correspondence is kept as long as needed to respond, maintain support history, protect legal rights, and meet legal obligations.
  • Push notification tokens and subscriptions: the backend stores an installation token, platform, app version, activity timestamps, and followed football entity IDs to route alerts. A successful registration or subscription sync refreshes the activity timestamp. Daily maintenance schedules deletion of registrations inactive for more than 90 days. Confirmed invalid FCM tokens and their associated subscription records are removed when detected. Finalized or cancelled match subscriptions become eligible for daily cleanup once the scheduled kickoff is more than 24 hours old; this is not a period measured from the final whistle. Team and player subscriptions remain until changed, deleted, or removed with the installation registration. Failed FCM updates and deletions remain pending for retry rather than being reported as complete. These processes require the backend and relevant services to be available. Uninstalling or clearing app storage does not directly delete backend records.
  • Notification delivery records: pending match alerts are retried for up to five minutes; expired alerts are not sent again. Delivery and deduplication records are removed by daily maintenance after seven days. Database backups are separate from the active notification database and may retain earlier copies until those backups are rotated.
  • Advertising, consent, Analytics, Crashlytics, Firebase installation/session, and Android backup data: is retained by Google and relevant providers according to their policies, retention settings, and your choices.

9. Accessing or deleting information

TopScore has no end-user account to delete.

  • To delete local preferences and cache, use Android Settings → Apps → TopScore → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall TopScore.
  • Manage or delete Android device backups through your Android or Google account backup settings.
  • Manage applicable in-app advertising consent choices through TopScore Settings → Manage ad privacy, and manage Google's advertising activity through Android ads settings and Google My Ad Center.
  • To manage alerts, visit TopScore Settings → Notifications. Turning the in-app Push Notifications switch off stops local alerts and requests removal of active server subscriptions after synchronization, but retains the device registration. Revoking permission in Android Settings → Apps → TopScore → Notifications blocks notification display; it does not delete backend records or automatically remove subscriptions.
  • Use TopScore Settings → Notifications → Delete notification data to request deletion of this installation's backend registration and subscriptions. Alerts turn off, favorites remain saved, and backend registration stays suppressed until you explicitly turn Push Notifications on again. Deletion needs a network connection. The app displays a pending state and retries while running and when reopened; the backend also retries pending cleanup. The app shows completion only after the deletion request and local token cleanup succeed. This action does not delete advertising, Analytics, Crashlytics, or backup data held by Google.
  • Clearing TopScore's storage or uninstalling the app removes local Firebase identifiers from that installation and stops future collection from that copy of the app. Previously collected Analytics and Crashlytics records are subject to Google's retention and deletion processes.
  • To request access to or deletion of information you sent directly to us, email tofa.du@gmail.com.

Because TopScore does not create an end-user account and does not assign its own persistent user ID, we may be unable to identify a particular person in ordinary API records without sufficient details. We will not collect extra personal information solely to identify you unless required by law.
